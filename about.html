About Jungle Brew

Our Story

Jungle Brew started with a simple idea: coffee tastes better when you're surrounded by plants and have absolutely no idea how many coffees you've had today.
We are a small local coffee shop built for caffeine lovers, plant enthusiasts, remote workers, and anyone who needs a quiet place to escape the chaos of everyday life.

What We Believe

  • Great coffee should never be complicated.
  • Plants make everything better.
  • Pastries are an acceptable breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
  • There is no such thing as "just one more espresso."
  • More espresso, less depresso.

Our Coffee

We carefully select quality coffee beans and prepare every cup with care.
Whether you prefer a strong espresso, a creamy cappuccino, or something cold and refreshing, we've got something to keep you happily caffeinated.
Come visit us, grab a coffee, admire our ridiculous collection of plants, and stay for a while.
